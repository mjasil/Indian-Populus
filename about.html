Our Story

Indian Populus began as a small editorial project born from a shared frustration with content that optimises for clicks rather than comprehension. We wanted to create a space where ideas could breathe, where writers could take the time an argument actually needs.

Today we publish independently, funded by readers who believe quality writing is worth supporting. Every article is edited, fact-checked, and designed to last. We are not a news site — we are a magazine, and we take that distinction seriously.

We are based across New Delhi, Mumbai, and Bengaluru, with correspondents across the country.

Independence

No advertisers, no algorithms. Just editors and writers who care about getting it right.

Depth

We take time with ideas. Our average piece is 1,500+ words, because some arguments deserve it.

Clarity

Expertise without jargon. Every reader deserves to understand what we are saying.

Equity

We pay all contributors fairly. Writing is labour. We treat it that way.
